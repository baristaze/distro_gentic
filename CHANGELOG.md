# Changelog

The latest release is listed here; every release's notes, older ones
included, stay on its GitHub release. Releases are tagged
`vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`; see `CONTRIBUTING.md` for what bumps which
number.

## 0.9.0 (2026-10-07)

The platform runs its own browser benchmark with a skill, and its base
is the engine at v0.8.0. Minor: the plugin gains a skill, and nothing
is reversed.

### Added

- `browser-judge-distro`, the platform's browser benchmark: its prompt
  names the repository's public URL and asks a senior architect of
  distributed agent platforms to judge the whole repository as a
  platform for fleets of agents in a closed loop, with adjacents named
  as kinds of system and the engine's report format; and its runs page.
- `distro-benchmark-browser`, the guideline's browser benchmark carried
  into the platform's plugin and bound to that scenario. A session
  whose page names a device of the person's, at any point, is stopped
  and not run. The check-in holds `results.json` to the schema and
  redacts conversation addresses, people's and devices' names, the
  person's other repositories and the Next section's product wherever
  they stand, and any name on a list the person keeps outside the
  repository.
- Run 1 of `browser-judge-distro`, on 2026-10-07 at sizes `m, m`:
  chatgpt.com 89 and grok.com 81; gemini.google.com declined, unable
  to reach the repository, and claude.ai was not run.

### Changed

- The base moves to the engine at v0.8.0, on the guideline's v0.52.2:
  a copy's access-line test bounds the line by the relay's measured
  start, not a fixed 300 ms. The spec and the lenses cite the guideline
  at v0.52.2 and the engine at v0.8.0.
