# Changelog

The latest release is listed here; every release's notes, older ones
included, stay on its GitHub release. Releases are tagged
`vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`; see `CONTRIBUTING.md` for what bumps which
number.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-03)

The platform's first release: its spec, the lenses and skills that
apply it, and its scaffold, the engine's at v0.1.0 taken whole, with
the platform's namespaces, processes, and checker built in. Minor:
every rule is added, and no released rule is reversed.

### Added

- The repository has the guideline's shape: its gates, its CI, its
  plugin `distro-gentic`, and the documents an agent reads (#1).
- The platform's rules are lenses, in nine groups a review reads one at
  a time (#2).
- The scaffold is the engine's at v0.1.0, taken whole and merged from
  the `scaffold` branch (#3).
- A review skill per lens group, a full review over the three layers,
  and skills that explain a rule and record a deviation (#4).
- A session's retention is a snapshot of its tenant's policy, and the
  sweep destroys its key in the tenant's key service (#5).
- A session's work runs where its environment is, and a tenant's loops
  are held to its fair share at the claim (#6).
- Trust: four answers on every call's audit, secrets by placement, keys
  by reference, content by grant (#7).
- The result is the evidence: runs carry provenance, validation runs
  apart from the agent, and one gate judges every run at the head (#8).
- Money: one ledger and one price source, limits by scope and window,
  credits only on a signed confirmation, rate apart from spend, and an
  anomaly guard (#9).
- The platform's agents: an engineer, analysis, a planner, an assistant
  that only reads and hands on, and a validation session with no
  agent (#10).
- A project binds a tenant's repository, and a session belongs to one
  from before it exists (#11).
- A workspace is a cache with its isolation pinned, its work pushed
  before it goes, and its egress an allowlist (#12).
- Intake: a routing table decides what an outside event does to its
  session; automations, playbooks, and knowledge, each bounded (#13).
- A workspace host enrolls once, advertises only what it probed, claims
  only its pool's work, and holds its owner's ceilings (#14).
- Models are a fleet decision: a versioned matrix, qualified fills,
  eligibility before resolution, and a tenant's key on its own
  calls (#15).
- A tool call into a customer's wall travels as keyed exec work, runs
  once on its host, and stops at once (#17).
- The platform's checker, `distro-check`, decides three lenses offline,
  after the engine's (#18).
- The fleet recovers through the sweep: an outage's sessions wake
  staggered, and a hold or a session nobody finished is taken up (#19).
- Acceptance judges the chain of evidence against a hidden suite,
  benchmarks keep scored runs against an interleaved baseline, and a
  sequential test stops only where its rule allows (#20).
- Events arrive through an integration's ingress, schedules fire once a
  slot, knowledge is recalled at start, and a park tells whoever can
  clear it (#21).
- Operators read the fleet through six skills and a dashboard of
  bounded labels, and set a share and a content grant through the
  operator plane (#22).
- A product scaffolds the platform's parts and moves its base through
  eight skills (#23).
- A viewer watches a session by a scoped handle, chat steers it as
  data, and a person takes control and gives it back (#24).
- The requeue's plan and the live read's end-to-end case read only what
  has landed (#25, #35).
- Each per-project policy reads the session's project: its budget, its
  secrets, its validation policy, and its protected paths (#26).
- A delivery reaches the tenant that connected its installation, a
  session's act carries its cause and hop, and a notification links to
  a route that exists (#27).
- Each composition root wires the money gate, the matrix, and the
  platform's ports, and refuses a quiet null outside `local`; one
  session in a project meets every gate end to end (#28).
- An automation's session starts in the project its action names (#29).
- A step the runner streams is read live through the API, from the
  shared cache, with model parts coalesced per stream (#30).
- An automation's queue, a validation's results, and a check's command
  template are bounded (#31).
- The sweep releases a workspace no run accounts for, on the runner and
  on a customer's host, pushing its snapshot first (#33, #42).
- A pinned session's workspace is prepared and bound on its pool's
  host, and its git work goes by placement (#34).
- A private repository is read with a fetch credential, the engineer
  pushes with a per-session token, and the workspace checks it out and
  delivers it with no credential in it (#36, #40).
- A notice the next loop has not read is never written over (#37).
- The platform carries no product's domain: a product adds its own
  work, hosts, and nouns on top of the base (#16, #38, #39, #41, #47).
- A delivery's checks run on a fresh executor in an instance nobody
  used, in the cloud or on a pinned project's pool host, and a
  validation session is platform work on the queue (#43, #46).
- A run that resumes right after a park keeps the workspace it
  took (#44).
- A command under open egress goes through its host's proxy and trusts
  its CA, and sees nothing else of the host (#45).
- An execution record names what the executor made, every trial
  counts, each checkout is fenced by its run's epoch, a pull request's
  body makes the forge fetch nothing off its host, and a branch held
  nowhere is cut from its last snapshot (#48).
